How tickets were sorted
Communication gap
The system behaved as designed, and a message or screen in the CSP app would have stopped the CSP raising the ticket. Tickets where the app had nothing more to say go elsewhere: a bank transfer already processed with a UTR, a payout partner retry, a device the warehouse never received. Examples: why a ₹750 proof was rejected, when the next ISP ticket is due, the 15-day window behind speed and optical power, carry-fee "starting soon" alerts. Fix with in-app explanation, push or belief-break messages, or clearer copy at the moment it matters.
Experience gap
The app is missing a self-serve path (cancel an auto-return, change the owner number or bank account, a scan fallback), or a screen blocks or errors without a reason (hidden order button, "device not linked" during a swap), or a label is confusing or stale. Fix by changing the flow or screen.
System defect
Wrong data or a broken process inside Wiom: manual ad-hoc deductions, stale device mapping, tickets not routed.
Ops / external
Things the CSP app's design can't fix: bank-side delays after a processed transfer, payout partner retries and outages, OTP SMS not delivered by the carrier, failed customer UPI payments, warehouse not marking returns, wrong links shared by ops, manual migrations and incentives.
Business request
Opting out of ₹750 Dominance, blocking leads, cancelling a recharge ticket.
Subtype
What the app failed to say (C1–C5) or let the CSP do (E1–E4). Every design-addressable ticket has one main subtype, tagged by Claude; the design team's corrections override it.
Feature asks
Tickets the sheet files as "Feature request" or "Request" (43), grouped by the capability they ask for. "Lead" says who has to build it: Design-led needs a new screen or flow, Backend-led needs a system or ops change the CSP never sees.
No issue / CSP error
Wrong number given, the CSP cancelled it themselves, a duplicate, or not enough information.

Every ticket gets exactly one lens. Where a ticket fits two (for example, ops delay plus silent UI), it went to whichever change would have stopped the CSP from raising it. The tagging is one person's reading of the RCA, so check any single ticket in the explorer above. Phone numbers are masked.
