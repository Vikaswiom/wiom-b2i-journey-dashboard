How to read it. Only call issues that fall in a bucket the 287 tickets use are kept: a bucket is the root-cause group (communication gap, action gap, bug or request) plus the subcategory. Call issues in buckets with no ticket (customer internet and outages, app-link and customer-number requests, unclear calls and similar) are left out and counted in the summary. Call issues come from Ameyo partner-support inbound calls, counted once per CSP + ticket note (the export repeats one note on every call from a number) and dated by the first call. Calls with blank notes, dropped calls and unregistered callers are left out of the tree and counted in the summary. Tickets are the 287 rows of the CSP Bug Data sheet in the same window. "Resolved on call" is call issues minus tickets in the same card: the two systems share no ID, so it shows how much never reached a ticket, not a call-by-call match. Where a card has more tickets than call issues (marked in amber), those tickets came in by another route. Call root causes are set per agent label (no RCA); ticket root causes come from each ticket's RCA. Phone numbers and emails masked.
