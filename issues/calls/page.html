How to read it. Calls and tickets are matched by topic (category and subcategory), which both are sorted into the same way. A call issue is counted when its topic has at least one ticket; topics with no ticket at all (customer internet and outages, unclear calls) are left out and counted in the summary. Call issues come from Ameyo partner-support inbound calls, counted once per CSP + ticket note (the export repeats one note on every call from a number) and dated by the first call. Calls with blank notes, dropped calls and unregistered callers are left out and counted in the summary. Tickets are the 287 rows of the CSP Bug Data sheet in the same window. "Handled on the call" is call issues minus tickets on the same topic: the two systems share no ID, so it shows how much never reached a ticket, not a call-by-call match. Where a topic has more tickets than call issues (marked in amber), some of its tickets were raised without a call note we could match. Root cause (communication gap, action gap, bug, request) comes from each ticket's RCA. Calls have no RCA: the "likely cause" in the calls list is read from the agent's label, so treat it as a hint, and it is not used in any count on this page. Phone numbers and emails masked.
